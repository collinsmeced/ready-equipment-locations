Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about renting lifts in Concord, NH.

Do you deliver lift equipment to Concord, NH?
Yes! We're based in Meredith and deliver scissor lifts, boom lifts, and towable lifts to job sites throughout the Concord area — including Bow, Pembroke, Hooksett, Loudon, and Canterbury. The drive is about 45 minutes on I-93. Call us at 603-279-7323 to schedule.
Can I get same-day delivery to Concord?
In many cases, yes. If you call by mid-morning and the equipment is available, we can typically get it to your Concord site the same day. For guaranteed timing, we recommend reserving at least a day ahead at rent.readyeq.com.
What types of lifts work best for indoor jobs?
Electric scissor lifts are ideal for indoor work — they're zero-emission, quieter, and won't mark up finished floors. We carry models with platform heights from 19' to 40', which covers most warehouse, retail, and commercial interior jobs in the Concord area.
Do you handle pickup when I'm done?
Absolutely. When your job wraps up, just give us a call and we'll come pick up the equipment from your site. No need to transport anything back to Meredith yourself — delivery and pickup are part of the service.
Do I need a license to operate a scissor lift?
OSHA requires operators to be trained on the specific type of lift they're using. We can provide basic operational orientation when we deliver your equipment. For formal OSHA certification, check with your employer or a local training provider.
What if something goes wrong with the equipment on my job site?
Call us right away at 603-279-7323. We have a full parts & service department in Meredith and can often troubleshoot by phone or dispatch a technician. For Concord-area jobs, we can usually have someone to your site within a couple hours.
